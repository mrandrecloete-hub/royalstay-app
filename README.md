# RoyalStay Intelligence

Run your whole property from one app: reservations, front desk, rooms and housekeeping, restaurant and bar tabs, a kitchen and bar board, guests, invoices and receipts, VAT, staff and payroll, backups and the Max assistant. Made in Namibia for lodges, guest houses and hotels.

It works like an app on your phone or computer. There is no server to keep awake: the files are served free by GitHub Pages and each property's data is saved on its own devices. A property can turn on sync with its own database so the front desk, restaurant and bar share the same records.

**Open the app:** https://mrandrecloete-hub.github.io/royalstay-app/
**Website:** https://mrandrecloete-hub.github.io/royalstay-website/

Honest limits: two devices that book the same room before they sync can clash, and the app flags it afterwards for someone to fix. Tax, levy, licence and records wording is to be confirmed with the relevant legal bodies, authorities and entities of Namibia. Nothing here has been reviewed by an accountant, auditor or security assessor.

Developers: see `CLAUDE.md`. Run `npm install`, then `npm test`.
